## 1. What Subcis is

Subcis ("Subcis", "the app", "we") is a voice app for small Quran
memorization circles, operated by Oogle. Voice circles connect small groups of participants for timed study sessions. 
Subcis is provided **as-is**, free of
charge.

## 2. Eligibility

- Not be banned from Subcis by us for prior violations.

## 3. Your account

You sign in to Subcis with Quran.com via OAuth 2.0. Your **Quran.com
username** is visible to other participants in any circle you join.
You're responsible for any activity that happens under your account.
If you lose access to your Quran.com account, you lose access to
Subcis until you regain it.

You may set a **Subcis display name** separate from your Quran.com
identity. Other participants see this name in circles. Choose
something appropriate — names that are offensive, impersonate
others, or violate these terms can result in your circles being
ended.

## 4. Acceptable use

Subcis exists to help people study the Quran together. The following
behaviour will result in removal from a circle, blocking, and
potentially a ban:

- **Disrespect** of any participant — insults, mockery, raising your
  voice in anger, dismissing someone's recitation.
- **Hate speech, harassment, or discrimination** based on race,
  ethnicity, sect, gender, nationality, or any other protected
  category. **Zero tolerance.**
- **Inappropriate, sexual, or NSFW content** of any kind.
- **Bid'ah** or non-mainstream religious content presented as
  consensus Sunni or Quranic teaching, in a way that misleads other
  participants.
- **Solicitation** — fundraising, marketing, da'wah-style cold
  outreach, or off-topic promotion. Subcis is for collective Quran
  study, not for distributing other content.
- **Recording or sharing** another participant's voice or likeness
  without their explicit, in-the-moment consent.
- **Disrupting circles** by joining and immediately leaving repeatedly,
  spamming the share button, or otherwise gaming matchmaking.
- **Impersonating** another person, scholar, or organization.
- **Bypassing** moderation tools (mute, kick, block) by repeatedly
  rejoining or creating new accounts.

## 5. Moderation

### Inside the circle

The **admin** (the person who created the circle) can:

- Mute everyone at once (members can re-unmute themselves).
- Remove a member from the circle.
- Hand the admin role to another participant.
- End the circle for everyone.

### Reporting

Any participant can **report** another via the participant's
options menu. Reports are sent to `subcis@oogle.tech` and include
the reported user's Quran.com handle, display name, room code, and
your reporter ID.

We review reports as promptly as reasonably possible. Outcomes may include a private
warning, an "Open Circle" restriction (still allowed in private
groups, where a host moderates), or a permanent ban depending on
severity and history.

### Receiving a warning

The app tracks how many "you've been reported" warnings you've
received on this device. Repeated reports may restrict access to Open Circle matchmaking. 
You can still join private groups
you're invited to.

### Anti-spam

We apply anti-abuse limits to prevent spam and misuse. If you hit the limit
during normal use, just wait a few minutes — these limits exist to
prevent abuse, not to inconvenience you.

## 6. Circle privacy

- **Voice is not recorded** by Subcis.
- We don't store transcripts or session audio.
- Other participants can hear your voice in real time and see your
  Subcis display name, your Quran.com username, and your gender
  (for "Open Circle" gender-segregation).

You're responsible for what you say in a circle. Subcis is a public
space — even a 5-person private group is "public" relative to the
people in it.
